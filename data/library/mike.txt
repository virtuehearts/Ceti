Mike

I called the first BBSs with Mike, one of my sister's friends, in
the mid-90s. Mike
was the one who knew the numbers — which boards were up, which sysop
was friendly, which number not to bother with because it was always
busy. We used a Commodore 64 and a 2400 baud modem, and we took
turns at the keyboard.

That is how you learned the BBS world: someone showed you. Someone
who had already been there told you the number, told you what to
type, told you not to tie up the phone line. Mike was that someone
for me. The boards we called were the first ones I ever saw, and
they are the reason darknet.ca exists — the board as a place, the
sysop as a host, the call as a visit.
